Your beach

Playa Cosón, a five-minute walk

The villa opens onto the first bay of Cosón — the one that reaches Punta Popy in a twenty-minute walk. Miles of golden sand, calm water, and the feeling of having the beach to yourself.

Morning runs

The bay's firm sand makes it an exceptional jogging route, at sunrise facing the sea.

Beachfront restaurant

The Ocean Grill at Coson Bay Hotel & Residences, right on the sand — beach chairs available when you order on site.

Snorkeling at the point

In the morning, at the Punta Popy point, magnificent fish glide through crystal-clear water.

Sand dollars

The bay is full of them. Only collect the white shells — the living ones stay in the sea.

The coffee route

Full-day quad ride · Samaná mountains

Rent a quad for the day and climb into the hills behind Las Terrenas: coffee and cacao grow on the mountainsides, and family fincas still roast their beans over a wood fire, in the pot — tasting included, with sweeping views of the coast. On the way back, a swim at Playa Morón, a wild beach at the foot of the cliffs.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Where is the villa located?

On the first bay of Cosón, in Las Terrenas (Samaná peninsula, Dominican Republic), a five-minute walk from Playa Cosón and a five-minute drive from the village.

How many guests can the villa host?

Up to 8 guests: three air-conditioned bedrooms (a suite with a king bed, a bedroom with a king bed and a bedroom with a queen bed), plus a sofa bed in the air-conditioned living room.

How far is the beach?

Playa Cosón is about a five-minute walk from the villa.

When can I book?

The villa is brand new: it welcomes its first guests from February 2027.

Are there power outages? Is the Wi-Fi reliable?

No outages: solar panels, a battery and an inverter provide around 24 hours of autonomy. The Wi-Fi exceeds 120 Mbps — to work or to forget about it entirely.

How do I book?

Message us directly on WhatsApp. We reply within 24 hours, in French, English, Spanish, German and Italian.
